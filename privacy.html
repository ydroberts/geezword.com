Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

GeezWord builds language-learning tools for Ge'ez, Amharic and Tigrinya. This policy covers the website at geezword.com and the GeezWord Games app (web, Android and iOS).

The short version. The games do not require an account and do not send your learning progress anywhere — it stays on your own device. The only personal information GeezWord collects is an email address, and only if you choose to type one into the Daily Challenge form. There is no advertising and no third-party tracking inside the app.

What the GeezWord Games app collects

Nothing, unless you submit the Daily Challenge form

You can open the app, play every game, and close it again without sending us anything. There is no sign-in, no account, and no profile.

If you submit your email after a Daily Challenge

At the end of a Daily Challenge you may enter an email address to save your score. If you do, the following is stored:

WhatWhy
Your email addressSo we can contact you about GeezWord
That day's score, moves, time and whether the run finishedSo the saved score means something
The challenge date, and the time you submittedTo attach the score to the right day
A random session identifierTo avoid recording the same submission twice

This is stored in a private Google Sheet that only GeezWord can read. It is not sold, not shared, and not passed to advertisers.

Anonymous counts of how the challenge is used

The Daily Challenge records that a page was opened, a game started, a game finished, and a form shown or submitted. Each is stored with a random identifier that is newly generated in every browser tab and is deliberately not derived from you or your device. Two visits from the same person produce two unrelated identifiers, so these counts cannot be used to build a picture of an individual.

What stays on your device and is never uploaded

Everything the app knows about your learning is kept in your browser or app storage on the device itself:

  • Which letters you have practised and how you did
  • Your daily streak and session history
  • Your alphabet choice (Tigrinya or Amharic)

None of this is sent to GeezWord or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it permanently — we hold no copy and cannot restore it.

No advertising or tracking in the app

The GeezWord Games app contains no advertising, no analytics service, and no third-party tracking code of any kind.

The geezword.com website

Advertising

The website home page displays advertising through Google AdSense. Google may set cookies and use device information to select and measure advertisements. That processing is Google's, not GeezWord's, and is described in Google's advertising policy, where you can also manage your preferences. The apps themselves carry no advertising.

Fonts and hosting

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives the request in order to serve the font file. The site and the apps are hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

Children

GeezWord Games is designed to be used by children, usually alongside a parent. It asks for no personal information in order to play — no name, no age, no account, no location. The only field that ever accepts personal information is the optional Daily Challenge email form, which is intended for the adult.

If you believe a child has submitted an email address and you would like it removed, write to us at the address below and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

  • Emails and challenge scores are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until the Daily Challenge ends and the records are no longer needed.
  • Learning progress is never sent to us, so there is nothing on our side to delete. Clearing your device storage removes it.

To see what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email yrussom@geezword.com. We will act on the request and confirm when it is done.

Other GeezWord apps

GeezWord publishes other applications, including Sirate Kidase Tutor, Geez Lissan and Tigrinya for Beginners. Some of those offer accounts or subscriptions and therefore handle information differently from what is described here. This policy covers geezword.com and GeezWord Games only; each other app states its own terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes to what we collect will be described here rather than made quietly.
