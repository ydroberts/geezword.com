Over Forty Years of Geez

Geezword grows out of decades of work bringing the Geez script and its languages to the screen.

1981–1982

The Beginning

Development of Geezword began in 1981, leading to the first Geezword software in 1982.

2001

Unicode Geezword

Geezword with Unicode support was completed in 2001 and became widely used in Eritrea and throughout the diaspora.

Today
